{\Huge\bfseries Preface\par}

\vspace{1.5cm}

This Master’s thesis was developed as part of my Master’s program in Communication and Media Engineering at Hochschule Offenburg.The work focuses on the development of an immersive, browser-based, collaborative virtual reality laboratory environment for conducting and interacting with physics experiments. The developed system combines WebXR-based Virtual Reality, interactive physics simulations, and real-time multi-user collaboration to provide an accessible platform that can be used through modern web browsers on both desktop computers and standalone VR devices.\\

During the course of this thesis, I had the opportunity to work with several modern technologies and concepts, including Babylon.js, WebXR, real-time multiplayer networking, browser-based virtual laboratory development, and the implementation of interactive physics experiments. Developing the system provided me with valuable experience in combining software engineering, immersive technologies, and physics-based simulations within a single application.\\

First and foremost, I would like to thank Prof. Dr. Dan Curticapean and Stefano Gampe for their  supervision, guidance, and valuable feedback throughout the thesis. Their suggestions and discussions helped shape the direction of the work and supported me during the different stages of the project.\\

My special thanks also go to Hochschule Offenburg for providing the academic environment, resources, and opportunity to carry out this thesis.\\

Finally, I would like to thank my family and friends for their continuous support, encouragement, patience, and understanding throughout my studies and especially during the intensive phases of completing this Master’s thesis.
